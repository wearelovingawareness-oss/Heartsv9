Tō Mātou Kaupapa

Our Mission

Tō Mātou Moemoeā

The Vision Statement

An Aotearoa where the mauri of the horse reweaves the broken threads of trauma, restoring tangata and whānau to a tapestry of sacred connection, dignity, and Te Tiriti-grounded belonging.

Tō Mātou Whakatakinga

The Mission Statement

To walk alongside tangata and whānau through relational horse–human experiences that support healing from trauma and restore right relationship with self, whakapapa, and whenua — nurturing lifelong resilience and honouring Te Tiriti o Waitangi.

Tō Mātou Moemoeā

"Ngā hōiho e āwhina ana i te hapori whānui,
mā te tikanga Māori"

Horses helping the community holistically, through Māori customs and values.

"Ko tā mātou kaupapa, he whakakaha ake i te hapori mā ngā hōiho, mā te tikanga Māori, kia pai ake te oranga o ngā whaiora, mā te manaakitanga me te whanaungatanga."

Our mission is to empower the community through horses, using Māori customs, to enhance the well-being of those seeking wellness — through hospitality and relationships.

We are dedicated to supporting rangatahi Māori, all youth, survivors of sexual abuse, and those navigating suicidal ideation by addressing intergenerational trauma in a culturally aligned and holistic way.

Through this work, we foster kotahitanga (unity) and community health as we guide individuals towards healing and self-empowerment.

We collaborate with doctors, psychologists, and psychiatrists to promote a person-centred model of care that encompasses the whole person — mind, body, and spirit.

Through systemic family constellation work, we emphasise the interconnectedness of family systems, exploring patterns of influence across generations to pave the way for collective healing and transformation.

Why This Matters

The weight being carried
in Aotearoa today.

15.6

Per 100,000 Youth

Aotearoa has one of the highest youth suicide rates in the developed world. For teenagers aged 15–19, our rate is the highest among 41 OECD and EU countries — double the US rate, and nearly five times higher than the UK.

1 in 5

New Zealanders

It is estimated that around one in five individuals will endure a serious sexual assault in their lifetime — and for some, such incidents occur repeatedly. Many cases go unreported, leaving survivors without access to care.

Roots

Intergenerational

Contributing factors include child poverty, family violence, child abuse, and issues of cultural identity — especially among Māori and Pacific Islander youth. We must address root causes, not symptoms.

Our Framework

Waiora

Holistic Wellbeing

Whakapapa

Genealogy

Waiora acknowledges the shared genealogy between people and their environment. You are a living expression of your lineage — ancestors, land, and stories. What is unresolved in the line can still be felt and carried.

Mauri

Life Force

Water bodies, rivers, lakes, and wetlands are considered to have their own mauri. Protecting the mauri of natural resources is crucial for maintaining the mana and identity of the hapū. Our healing draws from this living understanding.

Whenua

Land & Connection

Waiora emphasises the deep connection between people and the natural environment — water, land, and air. This connection is reflected in the belief that the health of the environment directly impacts the health of the people.

Understanding waiora helps in appreciating the deep cultural ties that Māori have with their natural surroundings and highlights the importance of preserving these connections for future generations.

Practice & Vision

The Knowing Field —
a space of love, radical inclusion,
and interconnectedness.

At the heart of our work lies a commitment to empowering individuals to make life-affirming changes in a way that is safe, expansive, and profoundly healing.

Belonging and being are deeply interwoven aspects of existence, explored through Māori perspectives that recognise our genealogical connection to the universe (whakapapa) and emphasise the vital importance of family relationships (whanaungatanga).

In our work, we honour these ancestral insights while addressing the complex terrains of identity, values, and belief systems in contemporary life.
